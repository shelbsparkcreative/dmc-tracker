Storage & Hosting on Google

🔒 Data Privacy Guarantee

Each device or web browser maintains its own **private local database**. Sharing this app with a friend will **never** alter your own data unless you manually import a shared link or file.

Option 1: Google Drive (File Storage & Easy Sharing)

  1. Save this webpage file (e.g. ThreadVault.html) on your computer.
  2. Upload it to your Google Drive folder.
  3. To open or use it: Download it to any computer or phone and double-click to open in Safari, Chrome, or Edge.
  4. To share with a friend: Right-click the file in Google Drive, select Share, and send her the Google Drive link so she can download her own separate copy!

Option 2: Free Live Website Hosting (GitHub Pages / Netlify)

If you want a permanent link (e.g. my-floss.netlify.app) that you and your friend can both bookmark:

  • Upload this single file to Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages (100% free).
  • You and your friend can visit the same URL, but your browser storage remains completely private and independent!

Option 3: Real-Time Syncing across Devices

Use the "Share Link" or "Export JSON" button at any time to easily sync your latest floss counts to a friend or another device!
